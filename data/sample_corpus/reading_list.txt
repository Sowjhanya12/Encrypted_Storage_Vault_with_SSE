Reading List

Cash, Jaeger, Jarecki, Jutla, Krawczyk, Rosu and Steiner, "Dynamic Searchable
Encryption in Very Large Databases", 2014. Source of the base construction used
in this project.

Islam, Kuzu and Kantarcioglu on access pattern leakage, and Cash et al. on leakage
abuse attacks. Both are relevant to the analysis chapter and to the viva question
about whether the server could recover keywords.

RFC 9106 for argon2, and NIST SP 800-38D for gcm mode.

Encrypted Cloud Vault - Project Brief

The goal of this project is to store personal files on a cloud service that we do
not trust, while still being able to search them by keyword. Ordinary encryption
protects the contents but destroys search; searchable symmetric encryption gives
both, at the cost of a precisely stated amount of leakage.

Scope for this term is a single-keyword static scheme. Multi-keyword and boolean
queries are out of scope and are recorded as future work. The deadline for the
implementation phase is the end of week ten, with the demonstration and the
written report due in week twelve.

Deliverables: a working two-process implementation, an honest leakage analysis,
a reproducible benchmark suite, and a demonstration that the server holds no
readable data at any point.

Report Outline

1. Motivation: why encryption alone breaks search.
2. Background: symmetric encryption, pseudorandom functions, and the searchable
   encryption problem.
3. Design: the trust boundary, key derivation, and the encrypted index.
4. Implementation: two processes, one of which is treated as an adversary.
5. Security analysis: the threat model and the exact leakage profile.
6. Performance: measured cost of indexing, uploading and searching.
7. Limitations and future work.

Section five and section six carry the most marks and should be written first.
